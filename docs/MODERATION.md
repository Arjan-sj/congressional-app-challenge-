# Community review

The privileged client review interface has been replaced with [anonymous community reporting and consensus](COMMUNITY_REVIEW.md). Every visitor uses the same Community tab; three agreeing reviews from independent connections are required to change a status. There is no registration, application or privileged-role workflow in the app.

Historical database role, author, flag and decision records and trusted operator maintenance tools remain private for compatibility and auditing. They are not a mechanism for anonymous participation, and visitors must never be added to those role tables. No historical users, reports, photos or audit records are deleted by this change.
